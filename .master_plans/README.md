# Master plans

One folder per initiative, holding the **public-safe** master plan produced by the
`marketplace-integration` skill (Phases 0–2: capability survey, decisions, the
step-by-step plan) plus the evidence it was derived from. A plan here is the
document the per-step issues are generated from; when a step ships, the plan is
updated, not superseded.

What belongs here: the plan, the surveys of the provider's own documentation,
verification records, cost research — anything derived from public sources.

What never belongs here: operator decisions that must not be published, credentials,
partner/app identifiers, and findings about the legacy application's code. Those
live in the gitignored `.private/` folder, which is copied into every worktree.
