The sync emit path applies #512's no-receipt disposition and the reconcile's recovery table (#1654 §1).

`applyAutorizadoOutcome` serves the single emit (`emitirPedido`), a batch chunk that shrank to one member (`processChunk` 4e) and the pós-EPEC transmission. Until now it read a sync `retEnviNFe` through the plain state machine, unlike the batch path since #512. A lote-level 108/109 left a fresh NF-e `enviando`. A refused #396 crash-window retransmit became `rejeitada`/`error`, so the next emit regenerated over bytes an earlier send may already have had authorized. A lote-level 100/101/102 without a protocol gave a final estado with no proc. A `protNFe` for ANOTHER chave was applied as ours. And the write was the unguarded `persistPatch`.

Now:

- **Our protocol is found by strict chave equality.** `retEnvi.protNFe` counts only when its `infProt.chNFe` is this chave; in 4e a miss is `null`, with no fallback to the reply's protNFe. A protNFe for another chave, a near-miss digit included, is ignored with a warning.
- **A reply with no protocol for the chave and no `infRec`** takes the same disposition as a batch member (`patchForLoteSemRecibo`), written through the same guarded persist: the per-member body of `persistLoteSemRecibo` is extracted as `persistirDisposicaoSemRecibo` (unchanged logic), under `persistPatchUnlessFinal` with `expectedIdLote`. Where the bytes came from is a new required `origem: OrigemDosBytes` (`'gerados' | 'armazenados' | 'pos-epec'`), fed by `TxOutcome.storedBytes` (true for the crash-window retransmit) and by `processChunk`'s `storedPaths`, now hoisted above 4d. **Behaviour change:** a FRESH NF-e refused with 108/109/113/114 is `rejeitada`, and `POST /api/nfe/emitir` answers 422 instead of 200 `enviando`, as `emitir-lote` already did. A crash-window NF-e stays an anchor on any refusal (656: its sweep consult waits `CONSUMO_INDEVIDO_ESPERA_MS`). A lote-level 100/101/102 without a protocol leaves the NF-e `enviando`. A 106 is no longer consulted inline. A doc a concurrent emit re-stamped, or one that went final, is left alone and reported `reused` with its live state.
- **A lote-level duplicidade (204/205/218/539/635) keeps its inline recovery**: with one member there is no fan-out and no reply smeared over N members, the two reasons #512 defers to the sweep. 539 and an `[nRec:]` marker behave as before. Otherwise its one `consSitNFe` (and the one for our protNFe's duplicidade inside a 104) is read through the reconcile's recovery table, `classificarConsSitDeRecuperacao` keyed by `motivoPorChave`: a final answer is applied as before; "still queued" (635 + 217) or an unavailable service leaves a #396 anchor (`aguardandoResposta`, `retries` 0, no `nRec`, no proc, nothing enqueued) instead of `rejeitada`; anything else, a denegada or a 217 on a duplicidade included, is a blocking terminal `error` (`terminalBloqueante`: 104 inside a 104 reply, else 103, with the real cStat as an xMotivo prefix), so the número is not re-emitted over. A consSit whose protNFe names another chave is such a terminal too. These three dispositions are written under the same lote guard.
- **pós-EPEC** (`origem 'pos-epec'`) keeps its handling byte for byte, including the plain reading of a duplicidade's consSit.

The 103-with-`infRec` hand-off and the 104 with our FINAL protNFe are unchanged (same plain `persistPatch`, same key set); a duplicidade or a 106 inside our protNFe now reads its consSit through the recovery table, as above. The new anchors are recovered by the backstop sweep's consult-by-chave branch for docs without an `nRec`, which does not use the recovery table yet (follow-up).
