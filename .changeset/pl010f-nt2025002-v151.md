Vendor the XSD pack PL_010f_v1.04 (NT 2025.002 v1.50/v1.51 + NT 2026.007 v1.00) and bring the rules to NT 2025.002 v1.51.

- **Pack:** only `leiauteNFe_v4.00.xsd` and `DFeTiposBasicos_v1.00.xsd` changed content and were overlaid (LF-normalised; the other three files in the zip differ by a trailing newline or not at all). The generated types move from 177 to 194 interfaces. Every emitted XML stays byte-identical except the per-unit IS below. `test/xsd/pl010f.test.ts` pins each change we rely on; run against the previous pack, 5 of its 6 cases fail.
- **IS per unit:** the element is `adRemIS` now (`pISEspec` before). The XSD always required `vBCIS` + `pIS` once the IS values open, so the per-unit path — which emitted `pISEspec` alone — was invalid under every pack. It now carries the base, a `pIS` of 0 and `adRemIS`, and refuses a per-unit IS without `uTrib` (`uTrib`/`qTrib` are one XSD sequence). The computed `vIS` is unchanged.
- **DANFE:** `emit/IE` is optional in the pack; an emitente without it prints an empty field.
- **Crédito 06 (retorno por recusa parcial na entrega):** new `tpNFCredito` member (`TP_NF_CREDITO.retornoRecusaParcial`). It keeps its ICMS (B25-80 exception), may and must reference the returned items by item (VC02-07 exception to 1042; 1038), and binds no cClassTrib.
- **v1.51 rules:** VC02-30 (1130) no longer applies to débito 07. The 1038 texts now say "este tipo de nota", since crédito 06 triggers them too; their keys are `refItemAusente` / `refItemAusenteNoItem`. Crédito 02 and 05 stay refused: their reasons (1145; 1132 vs 1133) read the same in v1.51.
- **Skill:** the NT 2025.002 v1.51 PDF is vendored beside v1.40, and the references now cite v1.51, including a PL_010f section. Also documented: the codegen does not propagate an `xs:sequence` `maxOccurs` to its children (`infProt` `cMsg`/`xMsg`).
