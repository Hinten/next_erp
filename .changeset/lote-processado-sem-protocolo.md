Cap the reconcile chain of an NF-e whose processed lote (cStat 104) carries no `protNFe` for its chave, and resolve that chave with one `consSitNFe` instead of re-consulting the receipt forever (#513).

Until now a 104 without our `protNFe` fell through to the bare lote outcome. `applyOutcome` reset `retries` to 0 on it, so the `MAX_RECONCILE_ATTEMPTS` cap never fired and `reconciliarNfe` re-enqueued the same `nRec` with no limit. `reconcileByRecibo` now sends such a doc to `reconcileLoteSemProtocolo` (`orchestrator/lote-sem-protocolo.ts`):

- **Every sighting counts** on the per-doc `retries` counter the 105 path already uses. The count is written before any further SEFAZ call. Past the cap the doc goes terminal with no call at all.
- **One `consSitNFe` for the chave per round**, the first time the doc is seen. An authorization, cancelamento, inutilização or denegação is applied as on every other consSit path (a denegação lands as `rejeitada`), stitching the digest-safe `<nfeProc>` when it is an authorization. A 217 ("não consta na base") becomes `rejeitada`, since the número is free. 108/109/113/114 leaves the doc counted, and terminal once the count reaches the cap. Any other answer, 561/562/613/656 included, and a `protNFe` that names another chave → terminal `error` that KEEPS cStat 104 (blocking re-emission), with the consSit cStat/xMotivo and "verificar manualmente" in the motivo.
- **A consSit breaker.** After a consSit 656, the remaining missing docs go terminal with no call. After an unavailable service (a consSit 108/109/113/114 or a transport failure), they are counted with no call. An outage costs one consSit per round, not one per chave. An XSD/XML failure counts only its own doc and trips nothing, since it can be deterministic for one chave. The breaker spans one lote on the task path. In a sweep, a 656 spans every lote of the same filial, since the 656 throttle is per CNPJ+IP, while an unavailable service spans only the filial's lotes at the SAME authorizer (home SEFAZ for tpEmis 1/4, SVC-AN for 6, SVC-RS for 7), so an outage at one authorizer does not stop the consSit at another. `reconcileByRecibo` takes and returns it (`bloqueioConsSit`). A breaker tripped by a reconcile that then throws is not carried to the next lote or the queue retry (follow-up).
- **Every write goes through `persistPatchUnlessFinal` under a `PersistGuard`** that re-checks, inside the transaction, the receipt, the `retries` the write was decided from and an in-flight estado. A doc another task, the sweep or a manual verify finalized or counted in the meantime is left alone, never consulted again by this run, and tallied by its live estado. `PersistGuard`'s fields are now all optional; #512's `expectedIdLote` behaves exactly as before.

A lote-level reply that says nothing about the chave (103/106/107/108/109/113/114) now keeps `retries` instead of zeroing it, so an outage between two 104 rounds cannot restart the count. Only our `protNFe` or a final answer clears it. A non-answer never trips the cap's terminal, which would carry its non-blocking cStat: a doc at the cap stays in flight at the cap, and the next 104 without our `protNFe` ends it with no consSit (cStat 104, blocking), or the next 105 with cStat 105, so a chave never gets more than `MAX_RECONCILE_ATTEMPTS` consSit calls. Two chains are still not capped, both pre-existing follow-ups: a pure lote-level 106/108 chain, and a 104 whose `protNFe` for our chave carries a non-539 duplicidade (204/205/218/635).

Every other write of a lote reconcile (the 105 poll, a lote-level non-answer, a 104 that carries our `protNFe` with its proc/anchor swap, the 539 outcome, a lote-level 656 and the attempt-cap terminal) now goes through the same guarded persist, on the receipt, the `retries` as read and an in-flight estado, so a concurrent terminal or another runner's counted write is no longer overwritten; the doc is tallied by its live estado. The patch each of them writes is unchanged. The 539 recovery's own chave swap is still a plain merge (follow-up).
